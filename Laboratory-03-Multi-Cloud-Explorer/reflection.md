# Mission Reflection

Completing Mission 3 helped me understand how different cloud platforms provide similar services while having their own strengths and features. The most impressive cloud platform for me was Amazon Web Services (AWS) because it offers a wide range of services for computing, storage, databases, networking, security, and other cloud requirements. I also learned that AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP) provide comparable services, although they use different names and technologies.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have several similarities. All three provide virtual machines, storage, databases, networking, identity management, and Kubernetes services. They also provide scalable infrastructure that allows organizations to adjust their resources based on their workload. However, each provider has different strengths. Azure is a good choice for organizations that already use Microsoft technologies, while GCP is particularly strong in artificial intelligence, machine learning, data analytics, and Kubernetes.

Business requirements strongly influence cloud platform selection. A startup may need flexible and scalable resources, while a university using Microsoft technologies may benefit from Azure. An AI research company may prefer GCP because of its AI and machine learning capabilities. A global e-commerce company may prioritize scalability, availability, and global content delivery.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that there is no single cloud provider that is always the best choice. The recommendation should be based on the client's specific needs, existing technologies, workload requirements, scalability, reliability, and future plans.

After completing this mission, my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio became more organized and informative. I was able to document cloud platform research, comparisons, client recommendations, service mappings, and a Linux investigation. This activity improved my understanding of how cloud computing services can be selected and applied to real-world business requirements.
